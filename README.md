# aiRose

## A 6-Month AI Mastery Roadmap for a Lebanese Film/TV Director

Built for Zahraa — Executive, Director, Strategist | 3–5 hrs/week | Free → Mid-Tier → Prestige

---

### What This Is

A structured, phased roadmap designed to take a Beirut-based film and TV director from AI-curious to AI-fluent in six months — on a realistic budget and a 3–5 hour weekly time commitment.

The plan sequences **free certificate-bearing courses** (Google AI Essentials, DeepLearning.AI, Anthropic, Elements of AI, Runway Academy) in Months 1–2, introduces **paid mid-tier production tools** (Filmustage, ElevenLabs, Topaz, Suno, Adobe Firefly) in Months 3–4, and culminates in **prestige programs and portfolio deliverables** (Curious Refuge AI Filmmaking, MIT Sloan / Wharton / HarvardX) in Months 5–6.

### Deliverables by Month 6

- A 1–3 minute AI short film for AIFF / Project Odyssey submission
- An AI-assisted pitch deck and look book for a real MENA project
- An Arabic-dubbed reel demonstrating Khaleeji + Levantine AI dubbing
- A documented AI workflow case study
- 6–10 stackable certificates from Google, DeepLearning.AI, Vanderbilt, Anthropic, OpenAI Academy, Curious Refuge, and MIT/Wharton/HarvardX

### Key Strategic Insights

- **Arabic dubbing and MENA localisation** are an underserved competitive moat — ElevenLabs supports Classic Arabic and Saudi/UAE dialects; Saudi Arabia's Humain is building Arabic-first AI media models
- **Pre-production AI tools have matured fastest** — Filmustage, LTX Studio, and Runway's Storyboard Creator now compress a week's work into a day
- **Video-gen models are not interchangeable** — the roadmap recommends running 2–3 in parallel (Runway Gen-4.5, Veo 3, Kling 2.1/2.5, Luma Ray 2)
- **Union and rights frameworks are tightening** — SAG-AFTRA AI guardrails, the Take It Down Act, and Content Credentials (C2PA) are non-negotiable for US/EU market work

### Lebanon-Specific Notes

- ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini work natively in Lebanon
- Stripe-dependent tools (Midjourney, Runway, ElevenLabs, Suno) may reject Lebanese cards — a USD-funded virtual card resolves this
- Sora 2 app is currently US/Canada only; Veo 3 access is geo-limited in some markets
- The Runway AI Film Festival allows Lebanese residents (Syria is excluded)
